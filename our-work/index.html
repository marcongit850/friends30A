Our Ongoing Priorities

Four areas guide the work along Scenic Highway 30A.

Protecting Our Natural Landscape

These places give Scenic 30A its natural character.

  • Coastal dune lakes, dunes, and beaches
  • Native vegetation, forests, and wildlife habitat
  • Heritage trees

Safer Walking, Biking & Connectivity

People walk and bike the corridor as well as drive it.

  • Timpoochee Trail safety and maintenance
  • Crossings, bicycle facilities, and bike racks
  • Trail connections and transportation planning

Keeping Scenic 30A Scenic

The roadside should match the character of a scenic highway.

  • Landscaping and roadside character
  • Wayfinding, signage, and gateways
  • Public amenities and screened infrastructure

Education & Community Stewardship

Understanding the corridor helps people care for it over time.

  • History, the natural environment, and scenic designation
  • Safety education for people walking, biking, and driving
  • Long-term stewardship with community partners

Past Projects & Accomplishments

Specific results already recorded for Scenic 30A. A year is shown only when this site states one.

  1. 2008

    Florida Scenic Highway Designation

    Friends' role. Friends played a leading role in the designation effort.

    Result. Scenic 30A received official Florida Scenic Highway designation, creating a long-term framework for protecting and enhancing the corridor's unique resources.

  2. 2021

    National Scenic Byway Designation

    Friends' role. Friends helped prepare and advance the successful application.

    Result. Scenic 30A received National Scenic Byway designation.

  3. Friends’ Corner at Eastern Lake

    Friends' role. Friends spearheaded its creation.

    Result. The site at Scenic 30A and Eastern Lake Road became a landscaped rest area for pedestrians and bicyclists using the Timpoochee Trail.

  4. Wayfinding & Signage Improvements

    Friends' role. Friends worked to create a more cohesive and attractive signage system along Scenic 30A.

    Result. A corridor signage inventory, removal of duplicate and unnecessary signs, and installation of safety and trail-etiquette signage.

  5. 30A Gateway Improvements

    Friends' role. Friends partnered with Scenic Walton and others.

    Result. Landscaping and gateway improvements celebrating Scenic 30A's state and national scenic designations.
