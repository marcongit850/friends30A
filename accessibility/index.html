Accessibility Statement

This statement was last updated on September 29, 2026.

We at Friends of Scenic 30A are working to make our site accessible to people with disabilities.

What web accessibility is

An accessible site allows visitors with disabilities to browse the site with the same or a similar level of ease and enjoyment as other visitors. This can be achieved with the capabilities of the system on which the site is operating, and through assistive technologies.

Accessibility on this site

This site is built to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. Pages use the site language, a skip link, keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus, and a single main heading structure. Photographs include alternative text. Color combinations are chosen for contrast. Motion is limited, and animations are reduced when a visitor asks their device to do so.

Requests, issues, and suggestions

If you find an accessibility issue on the site, or if you require further assistance, contact Friends of Scenic 30A through the contact form or by mail at 877 N County Hwy 393, Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459.
